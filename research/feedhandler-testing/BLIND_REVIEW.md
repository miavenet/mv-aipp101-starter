# Independent accuracy review

Review date: 2026-09-19. Reviewed the initial research draft before author corrections.

## Method and independence

I received the research directory and an accuracy-audit assignment, without the author's conversation or reasoning. I read README, ANALYSIS, SOURCES, SCENARIOS, HARNESS, VALIDATION and the download manifest. I did not modify those files, implement a handler, execute vendor software, connect to an exchange or run certification tests.

I independently opened primary-source web pages/PDFs, extracted cached original PDFs with `pypdf`, inspected workbook OOXML and QuickFIX definitions, and read the extracted Global OTC DOCX content. I checked all 17 successfully cached artifacts against the manifest: every recorded byte count and SHA-256 matched. I also independently downloaded the HKEX workbook and both NYSE sample artifacts: HTTP 200, with bytes identical to the cache. Cached bytes were used for additional content inspection; this is not a claim that every URL was freshly downloaded independently.

## Findings

### R1 — Low: incorrect bibliographic title for Nasdaq source

SOURCES.md S08 labels the linked document “ISE replatform Member Simulation Guide.” Its actual cover reads **ISE Replatform Protocol Release Notes**, **version 1.5, April 24, 2017**. “Member Simulation Guide” appears in browser metadata, which is not the document's cover title. The body also carries an older December 2016 date; use the explicit cover revision/date and mention the mixed dating only if needed. Correct the source title and add the revision/date. The cited recovery passage remains valid, so this does not change the research conclusion. [Nasdaq original PDF](https://www.nasdaq.com/docs/ISEReplatformReleaseNotes.pdf).

### R2 — Low: “implementation-ready” overstates scenario completeness

README.md describes SCENARIOS.md as “26 proposed implementation-ready cases.” The catalogue instead contains scenario designs requiring additional fixture bytes/encoder inputs, selected policies, reviewed checkpoints and thresholds. SCENARIOS.md explicitly labels its worked example a logical fixture, while HARNESS.md makes a fully specified profile and minimal corpus the first future deliverable. Replace “implementation-ready” with “proposed scenario outlines” or similar. This is an internal accuracy issue about deliverable readiness, not an unsupported exchange claim.

No high- or medium-severity factual finding was identified.

## Verdict and limitations

**Pass with two minor corrections.** The central conclusion is supported: public venue-specific procedures, expected-result artifacts and interactive recovery-testing patterns exist. The research does not establish a complete public Pillar conformance suite, free service access or operational certification, and correctly says so.

This audit validates research accuracy and provenance, not executable-test correctness. I did not check every numeric workbook answer, fully decode the NYSE binary, prove binary/text equivalence, verify all vendor access terms, inspect every diagram visually, or exhaustively enumerate every available public suite. Current/upcoming labels and downloadable URLs remain mutable. Browser-readable CME content was checked independently; the author's historical direct-download 403 observations were not independently reproduced. Excluded discovery leads were not re-investigated because conclusions do not depend on them.
